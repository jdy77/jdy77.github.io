Hello, Welcome!

My name is Dayun Ju, an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Yonsei University. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Yonsei University in 2025.

I am a member of the Medical Imaging & Computer Vision Lab, advised by Professor Seong Jae Hwang. My current research focuses on VideoLLMs, with a particular emphasis on egocentric video understanding.

My research interests include computer vision (VideoLLMs, multimodal learning, semantic segmentation, and representation learning) and medical imaging (medical image generation and segmentation).

🚀 I am currently looking for research internship opportunities! Feel free to reach out via email.
